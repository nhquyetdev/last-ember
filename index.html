LAST EMBER

co-op horror · 1–4 players · v

How to survive
  • Find the car parts hidden in the dark forest and bring them to the car beside the bonfire.
  • Feed the bonfire with wood. Smaller fire = smaller safe circle. If it dies, everyone dies.
  • Monsters can't enter the firelight. Outside it they roam, hunt, search, and cut off your way back.
  • Light a wood as a torch at the fire to see further — but they see you from far away too.
  • Normal: nothing hunts you until someone picks up the first car part. Then it wakes up.
  • Monsters: Crawler (fast) · Weeper (freezes while you look at it) · Antlered (huge, turns slowly, so zig-zag) · Mimic (looks and talks like a teammate, but has no colored ring) · Many-Eyes (sees far, calls the others).
  • Running makes noise. Each player holds 2 items (wood or part).
  • You can only chat while standing in the firelight.

PC: WASD move · Shift run · E use / pick up / throw wood / revive (hold) · F torch · G drop · 1/2 select slot · Enter chat · Space next spectate
Phone: left stick to move, buttons on the right.
